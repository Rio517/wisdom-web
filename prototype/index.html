A closer look

A head start is not the same as a shortcut.

Imagine you have practiced tennis and decide to try another racket sport. Some things may feel familiar. Other things work differently and need new practice.

Research on transfer between sports is limited. It does not show that tennis makes every racket sport easy to learn.
