Design study 002 · v02 · prototypes, not the site

Three directions for the home page and the lesson cover

Same words, same paths, three ideas. Each page has a Home and a Lesson cover view (the toolbar at the bottom left switches them; Reset plays it again). Links go to the site on port 4600.

  1. A · Stage

    One big sentence in the middle of a faint field. On click the lines lift away, one after another, and the paths grow out of the Beginning dot to fill the screen.

  2. B · Left third

    The opening on the left, the field waiting on the right. On click the words slide off to the left, the field glides to the middle, and the paths grow.

  3. C · Words into dots

    The sentence is the start: every word shrinks to a dot, the dots gather into the Beginning, and the paths grow out of it.
