## Story

### Chapter · Small choices

#### 01 want · Wanting and choosing.

Maya is eight, and she wants to be good at soccer. But she can’t choose to be good today.

She can choose something smaller. Practice is at four o’clock, and she can go.

Picture: Maya with her ball at the start of a path. Far down the path, small and pale, a flag says what she wants. Right at her feet, the first footprint is only an outline: the choice she can make today. A small gray shape sits by the bench.

- far: Good at soccer
- near: Practice at four

#### 02 again · Again and again.

So she goes. The next week she goes again, even in the rain.

At practice, she listens to the coach and tries hard. At home, she kicks her ball against the wall.

Each of these is one small choice.

Picture: one ground line with four moments of Maya on it, joined by her footprints: the field, the rain, the cones, the wall at home.

- m1: Practice
- m2: Practice in the rain
- m3: Eyes on the ball
- m4: Wall at home

#### 03 quiet · A long, quiet time.

For a long time, it feels like nothing is happening. The ball still slips past her foot.

Something is happening, though. Each practice leaves a little behind, even before she can see it.

### Chapter · Adding up

#### 04 map · One season, then three.

Season after season, Maya keeps choosing practice. From far away, three years of it look like one long line.

Let’s look closer.

Picture: Maya’s life on Lesson 1’s map, from birth to today, with gray paths she didn’t take and light green paths ahead. A box marks the soccer years.

- axis: Age
- born: Born
- school: Starts school
- team: Joins a soccer team
- basket: Tries basketball

#### 05 steps · Step by step.

Up close, the line is made of footprints. Each footprint is one small choice.

Most of them are the same choice, made again. No single step made Maya better. Together, they did.

Picture: the hero. The map stays behind, faded, and the box on the green line opens into a large lens. Inside the lens, the same stretch of line is a trail of footprints. Some carry the choice they were.

#### 06 builds · Skills on skills.

Something else adds up too. Once Maya stops staring at the ball, she can look up.

Looking up helps her learn passing. Passing teaches her where to stand. Each skill gives the next one a place to start.

Picture: three blocks in Lesson 1’s “builds on” colours, each resting on the one before, like steps. Maya’s footprints climb them, and she stands on the top step, looking up.

- b1: Looking up
- b2: Passing
- b3: Knowing where to stand

#### 07 maths · Maths too.

The same goes for many things. Nobody becomes a mathematician just by deciding to.

A mathematician chooses maths class. They do the homework, then do it again the next night. Years of small choices like that are what a mathematician is made of.

Picture: a second trail of footprints, someone else’s, with its own small choices, ending at a chalkboard.

- g1: Maths class

### Chapter · Something new

#### 08 basketball · A new ball.

At eleven, Maya tries basketball. Some of what she built comes with her.

She already knows where to stand and when to pass. Dribbling with her hands is new, so she starts that from the beginning.

Picture: Maya at eleven with a basketball, and Lesson 1’s basketball box. Knowing where to stand and passing start partly filled with soccer’s stripes; dribbling starts low.

- box: Basketball
- s1: Knowing where to stand
- s2: Passing
- s3: Dribbling with hands
- w1: Getting there · head start
- w2: Learning · head start
- w3: Just starting
- key: Stripes come from soccer

#### 09 late · Never too late.

Anyone can start practising, at any age. Starting later can mean more to catch up on, but the door stays open.

Real life is messier than this picture. Coaches, time and money matter. So does luck, good and bad. Practice helps, but it doesn’t guarantee a result.

Picture: the map again. A gray path turns green later in life and grows its own footprints. On the paths ahead, Lesson 1’s star for luck and diamond for a setback.

- late: A later start
- lucky: A coach notices
- setback: An injury

### Chapter · Takeaways

#### 10 keep · Three ideas to keep.

- **Big things start small.** Going to practice, again and again, is how big things happen.
- **Skills build on skills.** What you know gives the next thing a place to start.
- **Start any time.** Your next small choice is one you can make today.

Picture: the map once more, the whole line made of footprints.

#### 11 next · Coming next.

What Maya practises gets easier over time.

These days, she doesn’t think about her feet at all. So who is doing it?

Picture: Maya dribbling while she looks up at the field. The small gray shape is closer now, right behind her. Nobody mentions it.

- button: Next: Lesson 3
