## Common instructions

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Redesign this supplied life-path diagram into a genuinely modern, exceptionally art-directed standalone explanatory graphic. The earlier edit still inherited too much of the old hand-drawn infographic. This revision must replace the typographic system, annotation layout and rendering finish decisively.
Preserve the recognizable IDEA AND NETWORK COMPOSITION: small birth dot toward the left; a broad abundant field of paths not taken opening up and down across the left half; a single winding lived route with a deep low dip then an ascent to the current point near the center; a broad abundant field of branching future paths spreading from that current point across the right half. Keep dozens of distinct paths and asymmetric rises and falls. Avoid a uniform symmetrical fan. Every path advances left to right; no loops. Do not reduce the network to six branches or a process stepper. Exactly two small plain dots, never numbers inside dots.
You MAY reposition and completely redesign all text and legends around the network. Do NOT copy the original oversized KEY block, handwritten letters, thick bottom arrows or hand-drawn callout arrows. Keep a thin but visible vertical current-time divider. Keep the small existing WAITBUTWHY attribution discreetly as a reference credit in the lower margin, not part of the title.
This is an art-direction study, not a website screenshot. No browser frame, sidebar, controls, cards or mockup device. Wide landscape graphic with excellent margin discipline. No numbered steps, age labels or quantitative axes. No paper grain, ink wobble, handwriting or watercolor. Render type as genuinely professional typography. All text must be readable and purposeful.
```
