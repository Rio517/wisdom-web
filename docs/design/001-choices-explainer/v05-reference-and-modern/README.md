## Review inputs and prompts

The [first-pass prompts](treatments-v01-prompts.md) constrained the labels and their positions too closely to the baseline. The results differed in background and material, but inherited much of the old infographic styling. Review identified oversized legends and pointer annotations, rough technical joints, and overly thick dimensional tubes.

The [refinement prompts](treatments-v02-prompts.md) give each treatment its own typography, annotation layout and rendering finish while retaining the network composition. First-pass images remain available as review inputs: [editorial](editorial-v01.png), [technical](technical-v01.png), [dimensional](dimensional-v01.png).

The second review found a meaning error in the editorial refinement: a dark selected route continued beyond Today. It also found pale past-path contrast and an insufficiently distinct lived/future hierarchy in the dimensional image. The [targeted correction prompts](treatments-v03-prompts.md) address those issues. Their input images are retained: [editorial refinement](editorial-v02.png), [dimensional refinement](dimensional-v02.png). The technical refinement retains its separate angular, dark-ground treatment without an additional correction pass.

## Comparison visual review

All three comparison images were visually inspected after their last comparison-stage generation. This is an internal visual review, not production approval. They have abundant paths, a beginning distinct from the reader's age, a visible Today boundary and no numbered dots. The baseline remains the composition reference, not an exact geometric match for the modern images.

- **Editorial:** strongest reading-surface candidate. The corrected dark lived route stops at Today, future routes share the sage treatment, and past alternatives are more visible. Serif headline and flat linework provide a substantially different finish from the first ink studies. Generated crossings and small discontinuities still need deliberate geometry in implementation.
- **Technical:** clearly distinct dark-ground, sans-serif/monospace treatment with angular routes and a mint lived path. Fine lines remain a legibility risk when scaled down; this is not a verified interactive rendering.
- **Dimensional:** slender metal-like relief and contact shadows replace the thick first-pass tubes. The dark lived route stops at Today; futures are no longer individually selected. Color separation between pale metal and sage is subtle, so this is the less direct explanatory option and would need stronger state distinction in production.

The sculptural/dimensional direction is selected for refinement. The earlier editorial recommendation is not the selected direction. The comparison contains two review-driven improvement stages across the set; editorial and dimensional received both, while technical needed only the first revision. All stored PNGs are unchanged copies of their generated outputs.

## Selected-direction refinement

The [dimensional v03 image](dimensional-v03.png) is the input for [v04's exact prompt](dimensional-v04-prompts.md). The requested changes are even, slightly thicker gray strokes; a smaller key at the top right; Today at the bottom; more branching in the available vertical space; and gray alternatives continuing beyond the divider above and below green futures, fading before the page edges. Gray routes beyond Today represent alternatives not taken, not additional currently available choices. This is a conceptual distinction, not a measured opportunity count.

All three new revisions were generated with built-in ImageGen, visually inspected and stored unchanged. The v06 image confirms the requested key and Today placement, gray alternatives continuing above and below green futures, greater use of vertical space and soft edge fading. The gray network is more consistent than v03 but still retains subtle light/dark relief edges after the targeted edits. Do not describe this raster as perfectly flat or fully uniform. The fading is a viewport mask, not a claim that choices vanish near a page edge. The composition is accepted for a flat code pass; the relief treatment is not approved for the interface.

Any chosen direction requires precise code-native geometry and readable semantic labels in the later interactive implementation. The original reference and visible credit remain available for provenance; production reuse and licensing are not established by this study.
