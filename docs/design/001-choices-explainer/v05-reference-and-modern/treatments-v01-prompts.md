# Post-baseline treatments — exact prompts

Date: 2026-09-07. Mode: three separate built-in ImageGen style-transfer calls. Edit target: [baseline redraw](baseline-v01.png).

Each call concatenates the common prompt with one treatment. These are exploratory art directions, not pixel-matched vector geometry or implemented UI.

## Common prompt

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Input image 1 is the baseline diagram to restyle. Create ONE standalone modern diagram, not a website or a grid of options.
Keep its underlying composition and recognizable branch routing: birth dot at left; many untaken paths spreading across the left half; one winding lived path to a point on the central TODAY divider; many possible future paths spreading from that point across the right half. Keep the original rises, dips and broad irregular branching areas. Preserve the approximate number, density and locations of branches, the chosen path's distinctive low dip then ascent, and the two plain unnumbered dots. No extra nodes or numbered badges. No all-green single fan.
Retain the same explanatory labels and legend meanings, with exact wording: "KEY"; "LIFE PATHS CLOSED TO YOU"; "LIFE PATHS OPEN TO YOU"; "TODAY"; "you're born"; "your life path"; "your life, today"; "THE PAST"; "THE FUTURE". Keep their spatial relationship to the diagram. Retain the small existing attribution discreetly. Do not add claims, logos, captions or decorations.
This is a MATERIAL CHANGE OF DESIGN TREATMENT. No rough pen strokes, sketching, watercolor, paper grain, botanical drawing, organic ink texture or irregular stroke thickness. Preserve expressive path shapes but execute them with precise geometry. No metaphorical leaves, rivers, trees, human figures or scenes. Keep the whole diagram visible in approximately the same wide aspect ratio.

```

## Modern editorial

```text
Treatment: crisp flat vector-style editorial design on pure white. Use confident clean charcoal paths on the left, a deep emerald lived route, and green future paths. Flat uniform solid strokes with immaculate antialiasing, gentle smooth curves and no wobble or distress. Route width medium, not fragile hairlines; the underlying winding geometry stays faithful. Fine neutral-gray vertical Today rule. Replace handwritten typography with exceptionally clean contemporary sans-serif, strong hierarchy, carefully aligned legend and understated pointer callouts. Flat square color swatches, no shadows, no gradients, no ornamental borders. Restrained high-end information design: the visual difference from the supplied baseline should be immediately obvious in precision, typography and finish.
```

## Night-mode technical

```text
Treatment: premium night-mode technical visualization on a completely flat very dark midnight/navy background. Delicate, razor-sharp precisely routed solid lines with clearly visible cool slate-gray untaken branches; the lived route and possible future branches are luminous mint/teal ink, without blur or neon bloom. The lived route can be slightly brighter. Original irregular path geometry remains; do not turn this into an orthogonal circuit board, subway diagram or grid. The vertical Today divider is a fine pale rule. Small precise geometric sans-serif annotations and compact monospaced section captions, carefully spaced and fully readable. White/muted pale type except mint path callouts. No numbered nodes, gradients, starfield, particles, sci-fi chrome, HUD panels or dashboard widgets. The result should feel quiet, exact and technically sophisticated, distinctly unlike a white editorial illustration.
```

## Sculpted dimensional

```text
Treatment: sophisticated near-orthographic 3D line sculpture on a pristine matte off-white plane. Reconstruct the original branches as very slender, perfectly smooth precision-extruded rounded filaments resting just above the plane. Untaken paths are matte graphite; the lived route and all future paths are rich emerald-green material. Use subtle side lighting, small realistic contact shadows and extremely restrained material highlights so the relief is unmistakable while the diagram remains readable. No hand-made texture, rope fibers, rubber toy appearance, thick tubing, glass marbles or candy gloss. Keep a front-facing near-flat camera so the two path fields, original route positions and Today divider are recognizable; no dramatic perspective. Thin freestanding gray Today separator. Typeset every annotation in flat contemporary dark-gray sans-serif directly on the plane, not floating 3D letters. Only the path strokes gain depth. This must look visibly dimensional, not like a flat green pen drawing with a paper background.
```
