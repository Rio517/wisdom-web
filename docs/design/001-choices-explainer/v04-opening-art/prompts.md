# Opening abundance studies — exact prompts

Mode: built-in ImageGen, four separate reference-based calls. These are static visual studies, not implemented interface assets.

Reference: [supplied life-path image](https://i.redd.it/0ica4htbtm671.jpg). The original was opened and visually inspected before generation. It guides branching and composition; its captions and time divider are not included in these opening-state studies. The reference itself is not copied into this repository.

Each call uses the common prompt followed verbatim by its corresponding direction below, with the supplied image as reference input.

## Common prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story / infographic-diagram.
Asset: one standalone wide landscape static visual study for a beautiful life guide, not a website screenshot.
Input image 1: the user's original life-path illustration, a COMPOSITION AND BRANCHING REFERENCE. Reinterpret it as the opening state at the beginning of life.
Meaning: one beginning, an extraordinary abundance of possible lives ahead. Start at a very small unlabelled plain origin near the left edge, then branch repeatedly across the entire width into many dozens of distinct, wandering routes. Branches must begin early and continue splitting at irregular intervals; this is not one trunk leading to a late fan. Preserve the reference's uneven bends, rises, falls and sense of generous possibility. Lines are recognizable connected paths, not merely decorative parallel strands. The overall network opens out to fill much of the height on the right, with breathing room around it. Every route is a possibility, none is chosen or closed yet.
Remove ALL text from the reference. NO numbers anywhere, NO numbers inside dots, NO age labels, NO circles at junctions, NO badges, NO numbered endpoints, NO legend, NO axes, NO today divider, NO past/future captions, NO arrows, NO UI, NO sidebar, NO cards, NO icons, NO people. The origin is the beginning of life, not age eight. The audience is ages eight and up but the aesthetic must be sophisticated, quiet, editorial and timeless, never childish.
Use warm near-white paper and green ink. Keep meaningful paths clearly visible against the paper. No neon green. No literal leafy tree, no bark, no scenery. Avoid perfect symmetry, computer-generated combs, straight radial spokes, loops, spirals, or paths reversing to the left. Gentle crossings are acceptable. Wide 16:9 composition, high visual craft. Create ONE image, not a contact sheet or grid.
```

## A — closest-to-reference

```text
Direction A — closest to the supplied drawing. A substantial, irregular, sideways branching path network in confident dark forest-green fine pen lines. Roughly 45–65 distinct visible routes/endings, organized into several large irregular sub-branches, with open pockets between them. Hand-shaped bends may include soft angular corners, slight waviness and local up/down movement, much like the reference. Do not turn it into a rigid tree diagram or a symmetric leaf silhouette. Near-uniform line weight with just a little taper; monochrome green throughout. The visual should feel direct and alive, yet carefully drawn and extremely clean. Plain white ground, generous outer margin.
```

## B — botanical-ink

```text
Direction B — fine botanical ink engraving, without literally drawing a plant. A very broad, asymmetrical fan of about 65–90 intricately branching life paths, made of slender but legible forest/sage pen strokes with subtle human variation. Begin branching very near the left edge, and subdivide across the full width. The craftsmanship and graceful changes in curvature should recall a natural-history plate, but there must be no leaves, stems rendered as wood, flowers, labels or decorative frame. Keep gaps and forks visible. A pale warm paper background, delicate yet readable marks, broad irregular silhouette with no neatly trimmed right edge. High-end editorial restraint.
```

## C — wandering-atlas

```text
Direction C — an atlas of winding possibilities. About 50–75 connected paths spread from one beginning like an irregular river delta seen abstractly from above, but draw only thin ink routes on paper, no physical water or land. Use more wandering horizontal stretches, rises, dips, occasional soft angular changes, and unequal branching distances than A or B. Establish four or five broad branching regions with distinct internal rhythms, while retaining one clearly connected beginning. Refined mid-dark green with small tonal variation, medium-fine consistent lines. Strong legibility and structured abundance; not a subway map, not a contour map, no grid, no right-angle connectors, no uniform terminal alignment.
```

## D — open-horizon

```text
Direction D — airy contemporary ink-and-wash. Many dozens of clearly connected, gently undulating green routes grow outward from an early branching origin, and keep dividing into a very wide asymmetric cloud of possibilities. The main visual remains crisp individual ink lines; an extremely restrained translucent sage pigment softness can sit near some lines, but it must never obscure the topology. Sophisticated lightness and spaciousness, a quiet feeling of an open future, not fantasy magic. Slightly bolder connected foreground paths interspersed with thinner clearly visible supporting routes, all equally unchosen. No glowing dots, no particles, no bloom, no lens effects, no mist that hides lines. Do not make it a neural-network bundle or parallel hair. White dominates the page; nuanced ink contributes warmth.
```
