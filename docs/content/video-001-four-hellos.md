# Video 001 · Four hellos (script)

Status: draft script, v02. Created 2026-10-19; updated 2026-10-20.
A short video about greetings for YouTube (a 9:16 Short, then a 16:9 cut) and the site later. Maya gets four hellos at the school gate; Leo, who is glad to see her, mumbles to his shoes, and we see the gap between what he feels and what she sees. Storyboard: line drawings in the wisdom mockup shelf round `20261019-hello-wireframes`.

## Rules for the words

- One narrator, warm and wry. The joke is the situation, never the kid.
- Every thought bubble names whose thought it is ("Maya thinks"). It shows what someone **might** feel, not what the other kid meant.
- Keep each wrong move short; the better move follows in the same video and is replayed.
- Say the lesson in one plain line, on screen and spoken.
- Fixes never require eye contact: a wave, a smile or a name count.
- End with a question for parents and kids to talk about.
- Fictional characters only; every shot with people carries a small "Simulation" tag.
