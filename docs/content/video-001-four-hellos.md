# Video 001 · Ben's morning (script)

Status: draft script, v06. Created 2026-10-19; updated 2026-10-20.
A short video about first hellos for YouTube and the site: a landscape (16:9) video first, and 2–3 minutes is fine; a Short cut comes later and doesn't shape the content. Ben likes his classmates, but one morning his hellos say the opposite: a mumble, an ignore, a side-mouth "'sup" and a bump. We see how each classmate felt, what happens if he does it every day, and then a redo. Storyboard: landscape line drawings (v04) in the wisdom mockup shelf round `20261019-hello-wireframes`. Timings in the table are a guide; the long cut can breathe. Earlier drafts were "Four hellos" (v01–v02) and a separate "The bump" (v02), now merged into this one.

## Rules for the words

- One narrator, warm and wry. The joke is the situation, never the kid; Ben is likable and we see that he cares.
- Every thought bubble names whose thought it is. It shows what someone **might** feel, not what Ben meant.
- The kids react the way real kids do: annoyed, judging Ben, done with him. No self-pity lines (“Am I invisible?”, “I thought we were friends”).
- Each wrong hello is short and followed at once by a "Try:" card with the better move.
- Say the lesson plainly, on screen and spoken: the first hello is the first impression of the day; look up, say their name, make room.
- The lonely playground beat stays short and leads straight into the redo.
- Fixes never require eye contact: a wave or a smile counts.
- End with a question for parents and kids to talk about.
- Fictional characters only; every shot with people carries a small "Simulation" tag.
