# Video 001 · Four hellos (script)

Status: draft script, v01. Created 2026-10-19; updated 2026-10-19.
A short video about greetings, made for YouTube first (a 9:16 Short, then a 16:9 cut) and for the site later. Video is a possible later medium ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)); greetings belong to the future relationships subject. All characters are fictional. The labels show what someone **might** hear, not what a greeting proves.

Look: photo cut-out people in a paper and felt world, with felt graphics. Every shot with people carries a small "Simulation" tag.

**Idea:** a hello takes two seconds and says a lot. You can be glad inside and still send "go away" outside.

**Voices:** two sports commentators in a booth, warm and a bit excited, never mocking. Ray calls the play-by-play; Pat, the analyst, draws on the screen. Kids on screen speak only their hellos.

## Rules for the words

- Never laugh at Leo. The joke is the replay booth, not the kid.
- Feelings are fine; the gap between inside and outside is the lesson.
- A quiet kid has options (a wave, a smile, a name). Eye contact is one way, not the only way.
- Chips say what someone **might** hear, not what the greeter feels.
